# Big Personality: core question bank

Version: `1.0.0-official-key-2026-09-19` · checked 19 September 2026 · 120 items · 30 facets · five domains.

The machine-readable source is [question-bank.json](./question-bank.json). Item wording and positive/reverse keys are copied verbatim from the [official IPIP key](https://ipip.ori.org/30FacetNEO-PI-RItems.htm). IPIP places these items and scales in the public domain, including [commercial use without permission](https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm). The copyrighted NEO PI-R instrument is a different instrument; this bank uses its public-domain IPIP representation.

This table contains all 120 established items, not newly invented tech variants. The engineering and product context belongs in the interpretation and reflection layer, with that layer identified as editorial guidance. The separate sixth/seventh modules must not be incorporated into Big Five scoring or renamed as a validated six- or seven-factor inventory.

## Administration

Product-authored instruction: “Describe yourself as you generally are now, across your life, rather than only at work. Choose how accurately each statement describes you. There are no right or wrong answers. You may skip a statement.” This instruction is an adaptation; pilot it with the intended audience.

Use the following [IPIP scoring anchors](https://ipip.ori.org/newScoringInstructions.htm):

| Stored response | Visible label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Very Inaccurate |
| 2 | Moderately Inaccurate |
| 3 | Neither Inaccurate nor Accurate |
| 4 | Moderately Accurate |
| 5 | Very Accurate |

Use `null` for skipped/unanswered, never zero or three. Do not preselect an answer. Show items in their published interleaved order using `publishedItemNumber`; do not show keys or facet membership in the response interface. Save progress after each choice. Support a keyboard-accessible review screen and a visible skip action.

## Provenance exception to retain

For published item **58**, the [official IPIP key](https://ipip.ori.org/30FacetNEO-PI-RItems.htm) says **“Believe that there is no absolute right and wrong.”** Johnson's [2014 Table 1](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.psu.edu/dist/1/163537/files/2023/07/IPIPNEO120.pdf) says **“…right or wrong.”** This release uses the current official-key wording exactly and records the difference in the JSON. Do not silently switch versions. Any future reference norm package must check its wording/version and administration compatibility.

Published item numbers were checked against Table 1 (journal pages 81–83); the official web key groups items by facet and key rather than administration order. In particular, the A3 and C4 web-key ordering is not the published questionnaire ordering.

## Deterministic scoring contract

For response `r ∈ {1,2,3,4,5}`, score `r` when positive-keyed and `6-r` when negative-keyed. Sum the four keyed items for a facet (range 4–20), and all 24 keyed items for a domain (range 24–120). A display mean is that sum divided by item count (range 1–5). The standard IPIP scoring operation is a sum; displaying its mean does not change the underlying score.

Optionally show `25 × (mean − 1)` as a **0–100 possible-range position**. This is a linear rescaling, **not a percentile, percentage of a trait, population rank, or psychometric standard score**. A raw mean of 3 is the response-scale midpoint, not proof of population average. Retain full precision internally and round only for display.

V1 missingness policy: return a facet only with 4/4 answers and a domain only with 24/24 answers. Otherwise return `null` plus completion counts; show all remaining complete scales. This is a conservative product decision, not a published missing-data standard. Offer “complete missing answers” and “continue with a partial report” without treating skipped items as neutral.

Do not sum the five domains into an overall personality score. Do not compare the sizes of raw domain means to call one a person's “dominant trait”: scale distributions differ. Higher N means higher Neuroticism; label it correctly rather than quietly presenting it as emotional stability.

## Required bank/scorer checks

- Exactly 120 unique item IDs and published numbers 1–120; exactly 30 facets × 4 items and five domains × 24 items.
- Exact text, key and facet integrity against this frozen bank.
- Positive/reverse counts: N 17/7, E 18/6, O 12/12, A 7/17, C 11/13; total 65/55.
- All neutral answers produce every facet mean 3, domain mean 3, possible-range position 50.
- Respond 1 to every positive-keyed item and 5 to every reverse-keyed item: all scale means 1, possible-range position 0.
- Respond 5 to every positive-keyed item and 1 to every reverse-keyed item: all scale means 5, possible-range position 100.
- Every raw response 1 produces domain sums N=52, E=48, O=72, A=92, C=76. This catches the common reverse-key mistake.
- Raw responses 1 and 5 to A2 must produce keyed means 5 and 1 respectively; all four A2 items are reverse-keyed.
- One missing item makes its facet and domain incomplete without blocking other complete scales.
- Reject out-of-range, non-integer, duplicate-ID, unrecognized-version and nonnumeric answers; never coerce them to midpoint.
- Changing report copy, chart appearance, PDF layout or optional model output cannot change numeric scores.

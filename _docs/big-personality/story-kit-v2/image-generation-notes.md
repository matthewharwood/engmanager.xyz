# Artwork provenance

The card back was created with the built-in image-generation tool. The 78 fronts are exact-label, code-native placeholders, supplied as SVG and PNG. They deliberately share a coherent suit motif; they are not 78 finished illustrations. Each deck record includes an individual prompt for a future illustrated replacement. Keep stable card IDs when replacing artwork.

Card-back prompt: One portrait tarot card-back artwork, full bleed, 2:3. No text or numbers. Deep midnight indigo, antique pale gold fine linework, central eight-point compass star, orbit rings, small stars and mirrored fern sprigs; restrained editorial print texture with ivory and malachite accents. Original flat artwork, no existing deck imitation, no people, no mockup scene.

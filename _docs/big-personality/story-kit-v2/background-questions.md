# Your Story: 36 optional background questions

Version 2 · 22 September 2026

Every question is optional. Single-select means choose one; multi-select means choose any that fit (up to eight). “Prefer not to answer” is always available and exclusive. Self-description text is optional. These answers add context, not personality points.

Two additional optional fields: **report name or nickname** (short text), and **birthday** (date picker, processed locally for the selected symbols). A legal name is unnecessary.

Countries use the included searchable country/territory catalog, with “another place” and “prefer not” options. Race, ethnicity, nationality, language, and culture are separate concepts. These broad identity labels should be localized and reviewed with the intended audience.

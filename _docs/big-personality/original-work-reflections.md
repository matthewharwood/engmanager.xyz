# Original work reflections: unscored appendix

These 24 project-authored prompts are optional discussion material. They are **not** the seventh measured lens, are not part of the 170-item assessment, and have no scoring or validation claims. Personal Values in the assessment now uses the published TwIVI instrument.

Use any prompt to start a conversation about work conditions; do not attach numerical averages, personality types, or population ranks.

## Autonomy

- Deciding how I approach a problem, rather than only carrying out a prescribed solution.
- Having a say in which problems my team takes on.
- Being trusted to make reversible decisions without repeated permission.

## Mastery

- Time within work hours to develop skills I want to improve.
- Feedback that helps me understand how to improve my work.
- Assignments that stretch my current abilities at a manageable pace.

## Contribution

- Seeing a clear connection between my work and a benefit for people using it.
- Working on problems I consider meaningful outside my own career.
- Having a realistic way to learn whether what I build helped anyone.

## Security

- Being able to plan my finances around predictable compensation.
- Knowing that my role is likely to remain available over the next year.
- Receiving clear notice when responsibilities or expectations are changing.

## Recognition

- Receiving credit for contributions that might otherwise be invisible.
- Having progress toward promotion assessed against criteria I can understand.
- Opportunities to build a professional reputation for work I value.

## Belonging

- Working with people who include me in conversations that affect my work.
- Having colleagues I can ask for help without having to perform confidence.
- Feeling that my background or identity does not make me an outsider on the team.

## Balance

- A normal workload that leaves enough energy for life outside work.
- Control over when or where I work when the task allows it.
- Clear boundaries around evenings, weekends, and time off.

## Integrity

- Being able to raise concerns about a product's effects without retaliation.
- Working with a team that follows through on commitments made to users.
- Having room to question a requested feature when I believe it would harm people.

## Optional conversation prompts

- Which up to three priorities would you most want a manager or future team to understand about you?
- What compromise would you accept for the next year, and what would make that compromise no longer acceptable?
- What is one question you could ask a team to learn whether it supports your priorities?

Personal notes remain local and are excluded from public share links by default.
